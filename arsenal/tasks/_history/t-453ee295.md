Measured on an RTX 4060 against ComfyUI 0.45.0 with
`sd_xl_base_1.0.safetensors` + `pixel-art-xl.safetensors`, 2026-11-15, prompt
"a house on a hill at dusk", seed 1, the shipped `sdxl` preset defaults
(`steps 30, cfg 6.0, denoise 0.6, lora_strength 1.0`):

- **txt2img at 640x360 collapses.** The result is a tiled sprite sheet of red
  house fragments, not a scene. SDXL's native size is 1024; a 640x360 latent
  is far enough below it that composition falls apart.
- **img2img at denoise 0.6 washes out.** It follows the sketch's lines, but
  the output is pale pastel with no dusk anywhere — the prompt's palette never
  arrives. `lora_strength 1.0` with the bare style preamble of a fresh
  `pw init` workspace is the likely cause.

The pipeline itself was fine on both: snap, quantize and clean produced clean
pixel art from the img2img output.

Scope: find defaults that give a usable scene on a 4060 for both modes, and
say in `docs/guide/comfyui.md` what sizes the preset is meant for — including
whether txt2img below SDXL's native size is supported at all, or whether
`pw generate` should refuse it.

`requires: access:human` because judging the images needs a person at the GPU;
no unattended worker can close this.
