`pw generate <asset>` runs a provider; its images land in `inputs/` and the scripts normalise each into a version. `pw generate <asset> --issue <id>` repaints the issue's areas and proposes the result as a fix. Providers, defaults and the monthly budget are read from `pixelwright.toml`; `pw cost` and `pw doctor` work on the project
