**S11 · Dark theme**  `SCREENS › S11 · Dark theme`
> Oh, now I see the "Mark an area" button. No, it should live in the toolbar. And it must be clear when you are creating a new issue or working on a selected one. For example, if I click an advice is only it shown in the screen? Are those numbers always on screen? I don't dislike them, but they could cover some important pixels.
> I'd like the option to enable or disable them, to only enable selected advice(s), to know when I'm creating a new advice. I now understand they are only created via chat, that's why you put the "Mark an area" button there. It makes sense, but maybe I should be able to select multiple zones (with Ctrl+drag or similar) and delete them and then make it clear when I am asking the AI for those areas.
> So, I make a selection (that I can undo, maybe with an x in the rectangle corner?) and I send a message to AI to create a new advice. I can't directly create an advice.
> Not sure, maybe advices to be fixed should be on one screen and user's ideas or issues found should live in another one. So I can accept or reject OR comment on an advice (to continue a conversation and fix the advice or solution) and then I can create issues to be converted into advices by the AI.
> One thing I don't yet understand: are those advices corrected by the AI? I mean, can I see the current and proposed solution for each advice? Or the AI only detects and I need to fix them by hand? I think this is what makes me not understanding it. What are advices, what are issues and what are solutions? How do I get to the two parallel images screenshot? By clicking on an advice? Can then I Ctrl+click to more than one to see the final result? How do we pass from the rectangle-identified zone with the advice to the version without rectangle? Is there a before/after button that I can hold, like Google Photos show when holding a click or finger into an image? Just to compare them with mouse or click.
> Let's rethink all of these things.
> I think I don't like the font. Too big or too bold or both. Choose another one, please.
